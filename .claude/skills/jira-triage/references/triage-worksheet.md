## Classification checks

- Severity reflects impact, not how strongly the reporter asked.
- Priority incorporates urgency, commitments, and known alternatives rather than being copied from severity.
- Unknown frequency or user count remains unknown.
- A duplicate candidate has matching behavior and a compatible resolution, not only matching words.
- A proposed workaround is marked unverified until exercised or independently evidenced.
- Security evidence is shared through the existing restricted process, not copied into a public issue.
- Executed updates are listed separately from recommendations.
